Section 1Who we are

Lacuna Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17507110, with its registered office at Elizabeth Suite, 3rd Floor, 207 Regent Street, London, W1B 3HH, United Kingdom. We provide managed IT services to businesses, principally in the United States.

For the personal data described in this policy, Lacuna Ltd is the controller — meaning we decide why and how it is used, and we are accountable for it.

We are registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office under registration number ZA000000.

Privacy questions go to hello@wearelacuna.com. We have not appointed a statutory Data Protection Officer, because we are not required to — that obligation applies to public authorities, to organisations whose core activity is large-scale monitoring, and to large-scale processing of special category data, none of which describe us. Privacy enquiries are handled directly by the people running the business.

Section 2The short version

In plain terms

We collect what you give us when you enquire, book a call or email us — and some anonymous usage data if you agree to analytics cookies. We use it to reply to you and to run our business.

We do not sell personal data. We have never sold personal data. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

You can ask us what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we will do so within one month. If you think we have got something wrong you can complain to a regulator, and section 19 tells you which one and how.

Section 3What this policy covers

This policy applies to personal data we handle as a controller:

  • Visitors to wearelacuna.com
  • People who enquire, book a consultation, call or email us
  • Contacts at our clients, prospective clients and suppliers
  • People who apply to work with us

It does not cover personal data we handle inside a client's IT systems while delivering managed services. There we act as a processor on that client's instructions, and the terms are set out in the data processing agreement that forms part of our managed service agreement. Section 17 explains the difference and why it matters.

Section 8International transfers

This is more involved for us than for most, and worth being clear about.

We are a UK company. Most of our clients and site visitors are in the United States. Several of our suppliers are also American. So personal data routinely moves between the UK and the US in both directions.

For transfers out of the UK we rely on one of the following, depending on the supplier:

  • The UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the supplier is certified under it
  • The UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses
  • UK adequacy regulations, where the destination country has been found adequate

In each case we also carry out a transfer risk assessment and apply additional safeguards — encryption in transit and at rest, and minimising what is sent in the first place. You can ask us which mechanism applies to a specific supplier and we will tell you.

Section 10How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit, and at rest where the system supports it
  • Multi-factor authentication on every account that holds personal data
  • Access on a least-privilege basis, reviewed periodically
  • Supplier due diligence before anything is shared, and written data processing terms
  • A documented breach procedure — we will notify the ICO within 72 hours where required, and tell you directly where there is a high risk to you

No system is perfectly secure, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. What we can commit to is that we will tell you promptly and honestly if something goes wrong.

Section 11Your rights under UK law

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Be informed — which is what this document is for
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification of anything inaccurate or incomplete
  • Erasure — the so-called right to be forgotten, where no overriding reason to keep it applies
  • Restrict processing while a dispute about accuracy or legitimate interests is resolved
  • Data portability — receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format, or have it sent elsewhere
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and absolutely to direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis — which does not affect what was done before you withdrew it

Email hello@wearelacuna.com. We respond within one month, extendable by two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you inside the first month and explain why. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

We may ask you to verify your identity before we act. That is a protection for you, not an obstacle for us — sending someone's personal data to the wrong person is itself a breach.

Section 12Your rights under Texas law

If you are a Texas resident acting in an individual or household capacity, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives you the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal data, and access it
  • Correct inaccuracies, taking into account the nature of the data and our purpose
  • Delete personal data you provided or that we obtained about you
  • Obtain a copy in a portable, readily usable format, where processing is automated
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects

On that last one

There is currently nothing to opt out of. We do not engage in targeted advertising, we do not sell personal data, and we do not carry out profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. The right is stated because the law requires us to state it.

How to exercise these rights. Email hello@wearelacuna.com with the subject line Texas privacy request. We respond within 45 days, extendable once by a further 45 days where reasonably necessary, in which case we will tell you inside the first period.

Authorised agents. You may use an agent to make a request on your behalf. We will ask for proof that you authorised them.

12.1 If we refuse — your right to appeal

If we decline to act on your request we will tell you why, within the response period. You may then appeal by emailing hello@wearelacuna.com with the subject line Texas privacy appeal.

We will respond in writing within 60 days, explaining the reasons for our decision. If the appeal is denied, we will provide you with a method to contact the Texas Attorney General to submit a complaint, as the Act requires.

Section 13Other US state privacy laws

Several other states have comprehensive privacy laws with rights broadly similar to those in section 12 — including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Oregon and Montana, among others.

Where one of those laws applies to you, we will honour the equivalent rights on the same terms. Use the same contact route and tell us which state you are in.

Section 14Marketing

We may send occasional emails about our services to business contacts. Under UK PECR we may do this to corporate subscribers on the basis of legitimate interests, but:

  • Every message carries a one-click unsubscribe
  • We act on an unsubscribe immediately, and certainly within 48 hours
  • We do not pass your details to anyone else for their marketing. Ever
  • We do not buy contact lists

Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop service messages — invoices, incident notifications, scheduled maintenance and the like — because those are part of the service you are paying for.

Section 15Automated decisions

We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you by automated means.

One point of honesty: our enquiry form applies a simple rule that flags enquiries below ten users in non-regulated sectors, and shows a message saying we are probably not the right fit. That is a suggestion on a web page, not a decision about you — a person reads every enquiry, and you can reply and say why your situation is different.

Section 16Children

Our services are sold to businesses and this site is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email hello@wearelacuna.com and we will delete it.

Section 17When you are our client

Delivering managed IT means our engineers can reach systems that hold your personal data — email, files, databases, line-of-business applications. This matters, so here is the position plainly.

  • For that data, you are the controller and we are your processor. We act only on your documented instructions
  • The terms are in the data processing agreement forming part of your managed service agreement, which covers confidentiality, security, sub-processors, breach notification, audit rights and what happens to data when the agreement ends
  • We do not use your data for our own purposes. Not for analytics, not for product development, not to train anything
  • Access is least-privilege and logged

If one of your staff or customers asks us to exercise their rights over data in your systems, we will refer them to you and tell you it happened — because it is your decision to make, not ours.

Section 18Changes to this policy

We update this policy when what we do changes, or when the law does. The version number and dates at the top of the page tell you where you stand.

For material changes — a new purpose, a new category of recipient, a change of lawful basis — we will give reasonable notice before the change takes effect, by email where we hold your address. We will not change this policy retrospectively.
